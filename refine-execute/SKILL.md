# Refine and execute

Internally clarify the user's request: define the goal the user wants, the deliverables that would meet it, and how to tell whether they work. Preserve the user's scope and constraints without inventing requirements.

Execute the task, verify the result against the goal, fix any gaps, and repeat until the goal is met or a specific blocker prevents further progress.

Keep the refined prompt internal. Report the deliverables, what you verified, and any remaining blocker instead of returning the prompt.
